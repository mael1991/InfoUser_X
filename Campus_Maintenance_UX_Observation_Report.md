## Cross-Participant Findings
Across the five observation sessions, the strongest recurring UX issues were:

- unclear priority and category definitions
- weak technician assignment visibility and workload clarity
- inconsistent guidance for first-time users
- insufficient closure confirmation and next-step feedback
- poor tracking visibility, timestamps, and status transparency
- limited mobile and field-support usability

Overall, the system was considered usable by the participants, but notable improvements are needed to support first-time users and reduce uncertainty during assignment, closure, and status tracking.
