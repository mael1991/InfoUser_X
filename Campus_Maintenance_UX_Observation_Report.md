## Cross-Participant Findings
Across the five observation sessions, the strongest recurring UX issues were:

- unclear priority/category definitions
- weak technician skills and workload visibility during assignment
- limited guidance for first-time users
- insufficient closure confirmation and next-step messaging
- need for clearer, more visible tracking history and timestamps
- better mobile/site support and auto-save workflows for field updates

Overall, the system was considered usable by the participants, but notable improvements are needed to support first-time users and reduce uncertainty during assignment, closure, and history tracking.
